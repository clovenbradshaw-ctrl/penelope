Council Watch — the seal

The second weave on Penelope's loom. First weave: launches (LL2 feed, rung-proven R13). This weave: the Metro Council — meetings soonest first, each agenda in its own declared order — from the house's own Legistar machinery (legistar-surveillance-scanner), zero mouth calls. The window and the agenda are new organs, selftested, box-owned.

What it shows

  • council.html — the app: next 8 meetings soonest-first, countdown tick, agenda per meeting (tap to load, ordered by the item's own sequence), search across titles/files/actions, named gaps, freshness badge.
  • council-control.html — the control: same feed, same window, same countdown; no search, no hero, no agenda fanout. Layout differs only.
  • council-record.json — the record (EOT-shaped).
