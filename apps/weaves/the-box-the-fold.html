It's a tangible representation of the human capacity for working memory, a system that holds a limited amount of information in our minds at any given time. The weight of a memory, heavy as a box of forgotten things, often feels like a burden we carry. Remember Lebedev et al.'s (2004) study on monkeys? They trained them to maintain one spatial location, a task that mirrored the human struggle with holding onto the past. This is the essence of a divided existence, as we yearn for the better desires, the ones that offer hope and a brighter future. But, as the old adage goes, "The past is never truly gone; it simply changes its form." The heart, like the monkey's mind, can't be completely contained within a box.
