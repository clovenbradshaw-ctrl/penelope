The essay that discovers itself

an argument that revises its own thesis — the discovery is the meaning, and the trail is kept
THE PROCESS · the VOID is revisable. We began at claim 1 believing memory is a storehouse. Arguing forward — the wax hardens and crowds (Republic@522168); the cortex reconstructs (research@2782) — falsified the opening. Claim 1 and claim 4 are superseded ON THE RECORD, never deleted: the essay carries its own discovery, the way the EOT carries a superseded entry.

The essay (as it finally stands)

Memory is a storehouse; the wax preserves what is written on it. — the thesis we started with, now superseded.

The wax does not preserve; it hardens and crowds. What it crowds with, the cortex does not replay — it reconstructs, each recall a new making. So memory is a kind of storage after all — a permanent record. — argued toward, and falsified by the argument itself.

Memory is not storage; it is the work of reconstruction — the wax hardens, the cortex maintains, and forgetting is part of the making.

LESSON · we got 4/5 of the way through before the wrong idea fully surfaced. That is not a failure of the essay; it is how the essay earns its conclusion. The claims are born hypothesis; the argument supersedes them; the trail is the record.
